Can you judge the fact?

Each round gives you a written description of what happens in a video, a proposed fact. Depending on the round, you may also be given the criterion being judged. Your job is to make the best annotation decision. The situation is your evidence. Do not add information that the situation does not establish.

1. Read the situationSeparate what is actually described from what the fact claims.
2. Check the criterionA fact can be true and still belong under another criterion.
3. Make the callChoose Accept, Edit, or the appropriate Reject reason.
